Notes:
- qps = successful answers / total wall-clock time.
- p50 computed over per-host latency; falls back to per-call wall time when

- No public targets used; all traffic stays on 127.0.0.1.
- first-success is the upstream-compatible baseline.
- 'all', 'quorum', 'compare' are HyperDNSx additions with per-resolver provenance.
